Questions

Before you ask.

Still unsure about something? Send the question directly — it is a faster route than guessing.

How much does a project cost?

Cost depends on scope. A single-page website and a full business platform are very different pieces of work, so every project is quoted after a short conversation rather than from a fixed price list. You will always see the cost before anything begins.

How long does a project take?

Websites typically take two to six weeks. AI assistants and automations usually take one to four weeks per workflow. Larger platforms are broken into stages with a timeline agreed upfront.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. Video calls, email and WhatsApp cover the working relationship. Time zones are agreed at the start so review points are predictable.

What happens after the project is finished?

Every project ends with a documented handover and a walkthrough. Ongoing maintenance and improvements are optional, on a monthly basis or as needed.
