Artificial Intelligence Solutions

We develop AI-powered solutions that help businesses improve customer interactions, access information and automate everyday activities.

  • AI-powered customer assistants
  • Intelligent chatbots
  • AI customer support systems
  • AI-powered business applications
  • Custom AI integrations
Good fit if
Your team answers the same customer questions daily, enquiries arrive faster than you can reply, or you want AI in the business but are unsure where it pays off.
What you receive
A scoped AI solution — assistant, support system or internal tool — integrated with your data, tested on real conversations and documented for your team.
Typical timeline
Two to six weeks from discovery to launch, depending on data access and integration points.
02

Business Automation & Integration

We help businesses connect their tools, automate repetitive processes and improve operational efficiency.

  • WhatsApp business automation
  • Automated customer enquiries
  • Workflow automation
  • Appointment and booking automation
  • API and third-party integrations
  • Automated notifications
Good fit if
Enquiries, bookings and follow-ups are handled by hand, or information lives in tools that never talk to each other.
What you receive
Documented workflows, configured integrations — including WhatsApp — error handling and a plain-language runbook.
Typical timeline
One to four weeks per workflow, starting with the highest-value process first.
03

Custom Software Development

We design and develop digital platforms that address specific business requirements.

  • Corporate websites
  • E-commerce platforms
  • Custom web applications
  • Business management systems
  • Customer dashboards
  • Database and backend development
Good fit if
You need a credible website, a store that can take orders, or an internal system your team will rely on daily.
What you receive
A responsive build with database and backend, admin access, documentation and a handover walkthrough.
Typical timeline
Three to eight weeks depending on scope and how much content is ready.

Engagement

How the work runs.

The same four stages apply to every service, so the process is predictable from the first conversation.

01

Discover

We talk through the goal, the current setup and the constraint that matters most.

02

Define

You get a written scope: deliverables, timeline, cost and what is explicitly out of scope.

03

Build

Work happens in visible stages with previews, so feedback arrives while it is still cheap to apply.

04

Support

Handover includes documentation and training, with optional ongoing support afterwards.

What you can expect

Standards applied to every project.

01

Written scope

Deliverables, timeline and payment terms agreed in writing before work starts. No moving targets.

02

Working previews

You review a live version of the build as it develops, not a surprise at the end.

03

Clean handover

Files, accounts and access handed to you, with documentation and a walkthrough session.
