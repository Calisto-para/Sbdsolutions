Engagement models

Three ways to work together.

01

Fixed-scope project

A defined outcome with agreed deliverables, timeline and price. Best for websites, platforms and specific automations.

02

Ongoing support

A monthly arrangement for maintenance, improvements and small changes. Best once a product is live and evolving.

03

Advisory

Short, focused consulting for planning, vendor review or a second opinion before a significant technology spend.

Delivery standards

The non-negotiables on every build.

These are not upgrades. They are the baseline standard for anything delivered under the SBD Solutions name.

Talk about your project
  • Responsive layout tested across phone, tablet and desktop
  • Performance treated as a requirement, not an afterthought
  • Accessible structure, readable contrast and keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Security basics: valid certificates, safe forms, controlled access
  • Analytics and search setup so results can be measured
  • Documentation and a handover walkthrough at the end of the project
